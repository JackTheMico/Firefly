# 0001. Adopt Immersive New Eridu Street HUD Aesthetic

We decided to transform the Firefly blog into an immersive Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ) street-tech HUD experience rather than a superficial color-only reskin or an overly complex mini-game simulator. This balances bold visual identity (chiseled geometry, acid yellow on dark, hazard patterns, tape/commission metaphors) with readability, accessibility, and high performance as a content-first technical blog.
